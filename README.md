# Nabdh Tech

Mapping the female technical talent pipeline in Saudi Arabia.

A data visualization project that examines the relationship between women's participation in technical and ICT sectors and the goals of Vision 2030. The dashboard transforms official Saudi government open data into clear, accessible visual insights.

## The Question

How aligned is women's representation in the ICT workforce with national transformation goals, and what does the data actually reveal beyond the headline numbers?

## What This Project Does

Tracks the growth of women's participation in Saudi ICT from 2020 to 2024.

Benchmarks national progress against Vision 2030 targets and global standards from the International Telecommunication Union.

Surfaces the gap between rising participation rates and absolute workforce representation.

Presents every data point with its original source for full transparency.
